# NIFTY Sector Strength — project notes

Owner: Vivek (GitHub `vs916491-maker`). Personal analysis only, not investment advice.
Repo: `vs916491-maker/Nifty-sector-strength` (public). Site: GitHub Pages, signs in with the F&O Desk email + password.

## What it is
Daily sector-rotation website for positional cash trades (held weeks to months). Ranks sectoral and thematic indices by relative strength and drills into the strongest stocks in leading sectors. Long ideas only.

## Pages
- **Overview** – Nifty 500 (default) / Nifty 50 toggle; Sectoral and Thematic tabs (15 + 13 indices); Relative Rotation Graph (RRG, "option D" style, dotted leader lines for labels); ranking table; market breadth; focus sectors with top stocks; **regime light**, **internals gauge**, seller-exhaustion and distribution trackers; Rotation watch.
- **Sector page** (`#/sector/IT`) – price + averages, RS, score history, breadth, member stocks.
- **Stock picks** (`#/picks`) – picks from focus sectors, F&O filter, positional setups (breakout, tight base, pullback to 50-DMA), Next leaders, position sizing.
- **Track record** (`#/record`) – did top-ranked sectors, picks, setups and the regime light actually work.
- **Watchlist** (`#/watch`) – Supabase table `fd_watchlist`, shared with the Fundamentals Desk (the F&O Desk has its own separate watchlist).
- **My Trades** (`#/trades`) – private cash-trade log (Supabase `cash_trades`).
- **Run scan** button (red) – intraday provisional rescan using live NSE prices.

## Scoring (details at top of `analysis/sectors.py`, `analysis/stocks.py`)
- Sector score: RS trend 30%, RS momentum 20%, excess return 20%, trend 20%, breadth 10%.
- Stock score: RS rating 35%, trend 25%, near 52-week high 15%, recent RS 15%, accumulation 10%.
- Picks: up to 5 per focus sector, mcap ≥ ₹500 cr, 20-day traded value ≥ ₹5 cr, score ≥ 60.
- Setups: RS rating 70+, stop at swing low (max 8%), exit on close below 50-DMA or 120 sessions.
- Caution (added 6 Nov 2026; Rotation watch column + chip under Quadrant in the ranking): **Stretched** = leader (RS-Ratio ≥ 100) > 8% above 50-DMA, or 2 of 3 (RSI 70+, > 8% above 50-DMA, breadth down 15+ pts in 20 sessions while within 2% of 20-day high). **Oversold** = laggard (RS-Ratio < 100) > 8% below 50-DMA, or RSI < 30 in last 5 sessions and now rising. Backtest Oct 2024–Nov 2026, next 10 sessions: Stretched −0.5% (46% up), Oversold +1.4% (55% up). "RS rolling over" signals tested and rejected (no edge). Rules in `analysis/sectors.py` (`CAUTION`, `caution()`). Not on Track record yet (his choice).

## Main files
- `collector/` – downloads NSE end-of-day files into `data/` (indices, stocks, constituents, corporate actions)
- `analysis/build.py` – runs all scoring; `regime.py`, `turning.py`, `setups.py`, `leaders.py`, `validate.py`

## How it runs
- Workflow `update-data.yml` (Daily data update) – weekday evenings. Started on time by Supabase pg_cron (`sector-eod-1` ~19:20 IST, `sector-eod-2` ~22:20 IST); GitHub cron at 19:17 / 22:17 IST is backup.
- Intraday "Run scan" and cash-trade checks run from the **fno-desk** repo (`sector-intraday.yml`, `cash-trades.yml`).
- Shared Supabase project, token and scheduling pattern: see F&O Desk notes.
- Secrets: `SUPABASE_URL`, `SUPABASE_SERVICE_KEY`.

## Rules / decisions to keep
- Data: free NSE files only (Yahoo as fallback). Raw prices stored; splits/bonuses applied at analysis time.
- My Trades: totals at the top cover the current financial year only (Apr–Mar, by sell date) and start fresh each 1 April; completed years show in "Year by year". Closed trades are locked: no editing and no Delete button (open trades can still be deleted). Exception (5 Nov 2026): a trade sold by hand can be edited (sell price/date/qty) or reopened until the next weekday 19:40 IST check (`closed_at` + `trade_fix_until`, fno-desk `supabase/011_fix_closed_trades.sql`); trades closed by the job lock at once.
- **Turning-point cycle** (8 Nov 2026, `analysis/turning.py` `_phases()`, both benchmarks; Vivek's design): only one box is live at a time. Seller exhaustion --Confirmed--> Up continuation --up weakens--> Distribution --Breakdown--> Down continuation --Washout--> Seller exhaustion. Up conditions (3 of 4): index above 20-day and 50-day average, 50%+ of stocks above 50-day, gauge above 0; once proven (3 of 4 at least once) Up ends when 2+ fail for 3 days in a row (`UP_WEAK_DAYS`). Down mirrors them and ends only at a Washout. Failed moves: close below the bottom's low (lowest close of 40 sessions up to Confirmed) or a new Washout sends Up back to Seller exhaustion (event "Exhaustion failed: new low / new washout"; Trend continuation box then shows "Exhaustion failed · downtrend continues" with the down conditions until a new bottom confirms; Seller exhaustion's badge starts fresh, Vivek agreed 8 Nov 2026); close above the top's high sends Down back to Distribution. Shortcuts (added because Nifty 50 got stuck in Distribution May–Oct 2026 with no Breakdown): Washout while Distribution is live -> Seller exhaustion; Extended while Seller exhaustion is live -> Distribution. Stages count only while their box is live; resting boxes show "Resting" and why. Site: 4-step strip + "Cycle history", Trend continuation box (Up/Down/Resting).
- Distribution Stage 1 (Extended) uses the **3-month high** (`EXT_LOOK` 63, was 6-month; Vivek approved 8 Nov 2026): the 6-month rule could not see a lower high inside a range or decline (Nifty 50 Aug 2026 top was missed; now Breakdown 19 Aug 2026 at 24,078). Backtest Jul 2025–Nov 2026: no other signal changed on either index. **Range tops on since 8 Nov 2026 (his go-ahead after the 2-year backtest):** `EXT_BOUNCE` 5 (a 5%+ bounce off the 60-day low also counts as the strong rally) and `DIV_NEAR_HIGH` 3 (divergence counts within 3% of the earlier high, not 1%); together they catch Nifty 500's Jan 2026 and late-Feb 2026 tops but add a Jun 2026 whipsaw; Nifty 50 unchanged.
- 2-year backtest (8 Nov 2026; data backfilled to Oct 2023, `BACKFILL_START`; signals valid from 29 Jul 2024; page "Turning-Point Backtest" artifact): current rules caught bottoms 4/5 on both indices (missed the 7 Apr 2025 retest), tops 2/5 Nifty 500 and 3/5 Nifty 50; false bottoms 3 of 6 on Nifty 500 (2024–25 slide). Tested and rejected: `RECLAIM_DAYS` (bottom without washout: no extra turns, more noise), `BD_CONFIRM` 1 (same whipsaws, later) and 2 (removes all breakdowns), Stage 2 as an early entry (fires 6–30 sessions before the low, index then falls 3–8% more). Switched on after it (8 Nov 2026): range tops and `DOWN_CAP` (regime.py: while the cycle is in Down continuation, Green shows Amber, note "Held back by a breakdown"; Green days in Down continuation did Nifty 500 −1.4% next month vs −0.4% other Green). Observation: in this range market Green days averaged worse next-month returns than Red.
- A/D divergence (tested 8 Nov 2026, **left off by his choice**): near the 60-day high, a falling market A/D line below its 50-day average was followed by −2.6% to −2.8% over 3 months and a 5%+ dip in ~57% of days (vs ~7% when A/D healthy), confirming his view that a falling A/D line is an early sign of distribution. As a cycle rule it was mixed (`AD_UP_END` 3: Nifty 500 Feb 2026 Distribution 15 sessions earlier, Nifty 50 Mar 2025 too early) and it misfires right after V-bottoms (20-day look-back still holds the fall). Stage 2 already covers it while Distribution is live. Switches `AD_DIV`, `AD_UP_END` and `build(ad_market=...)` stay off.

- **Choppy market** warning (8 Nov 2026, Vivek's own rules, replacing the first "either" version; `turning.chop_series()`/`chop()` → `cycle.chop.state`; `chopTag()`/`chopNote()` in site/app.js). Uses the **50-day EMA**. Context only, changes no signal or light. **Choppy** (red "✱ Choppy market" + reason) = (1) today within ±3% of the 50 EMA and in the last 30 sessions never held beyond 3% for 3+ days (`CHOP_BAND`, `CHOP_HOLD`, `CHOP_LOOK`) and (2) the 50 EMA moved < 2% in 30 sessions or its 5-day direction flipped 2+ times (`CHOP_EMA_FLAT`, `CHOP_EMA_FLIPS`, `CHOP_SLOPE_DAYS`). Exceptions: (3) **quick push** — crossed the EMA and > 3% away within 4 sessions (`CHOP_PUSH_DAYS`): no warning while it holds; after falling back inside 3% still none while the 50 EMA slopes the push's way (his correction); ends on a cross back. (4) **Congestion** — last 10 closes within ±1% of the EMA (`CONG_DAYS`, `CONG_BAND`): amber "◆ Congestion · watch for break" instead; a close outside the congestion's range = break, no warning while it holds (ends back inside the range or across the EMA; grey note "Broke down/out…"). History: Nifty 50 choppy 29% of days (Aug–Oct 2025, Dec 2025–Feb 2026, Jun–Aug 2026), congestion 25–26 Feb 2026 then break, 26 Aug–1 Sep 2026 then break (his two circled zones).
